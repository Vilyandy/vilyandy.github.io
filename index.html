02 / Behind the lens

A little about Vivian.

Visual notesBali, ID

“I’m interested in images that feel refined, human, and just a little unexpected.”

Vivian Sung is a fashion photographer based in Bali, Indonesia. Her photography journey began in 2018, inspired by a fascination with magazine imagery. After spending three years in Jakarta building experience through portrait and event work, she moved to Bali for new inspiration and creative collaborations.

Her work centres on fashion editorials, portraits and beauty imagery—bringing together thoughtful composition, a strong sense of character and a refined visual style.
